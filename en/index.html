Accounts and cards

All your money, in one place.

Your bank accounts, cash, credit cards and loans, together. At a glance, you know how much you have, how much you owe and how your net worth is doing.

  • Net worth and its history
  • Credit used and what’s paid each cycle
  • The month’s income and expenses, with what’s still to come

Statements

Every purchase lands on the right statement.

SPNT builds your card statements with their closing and due dates. You know how much to pay, when, and whether it’s paid off.

  • Previous balance, purchases and payments, all reconciled
  • Chargebacks and recurring charges
  • Paid statements don’t change without your permission

Loans

See how much you save by paying toward principal.

Track your loans installment by installment: principal, interest and insurance. Before you pay extra, simulate how much interest and how many months you’ll save.

  • Full amortization table
  • One-time or monthly extra payments
  • Choose to shorten the term or lower the installment

Insights

Understand where it goes.

Compare income and expenses month over month, see your savings rate and which categories grew. The month’s pace tells you if you’re spending more than usual.

  • By month, quarter, year or a custom range
  • Spending by category, and what drove it
  • PDF report and export to Excel or CSV

Currencies

Any currency, at the day’s rate.

Bought something online or traveling? Enter the amount in its original currency, and SPNT converts it to yours at that day’s exchange rate.

  • Exchange rates updated daily
  • Adjust the rate, or enter what your bank charged
  • You choose your base currency

Transactions

What’s paid and what’s pending, at a glance.

Log an expense in seconds. What repeats every month adds itself, and you always see what you’ve paid and what’s left.

  • Recurring expenses and income
  • Categories, tags and saved filters
  • Search across your whole history

Your data is yours.

  • We never sell your data, whether you use SPNT free or with Premium.
  • Your data lives on your iPhone. If you sync, it travels encrypted and only you can see it.
  • It doesn’t connect to your bank or read your email.
  • No ads, no tracking.
  • Hide amounts and reveal them with Face ID.
