Questions

What everyone asks.

Does SPNT connect to my bank?

No. You log each transaction yourself, in seconds, and recurring ones add themselves. SPNT never asks for your bank login or reads your email.

Do I need an account?

Not to use SPNT for free. Premium needs one because it includes backup and sync; you sign in with Apple or Google.

Does it work offline?

Yes. Your data lives on your iPhone. With Premium, changes sync when you’re back online.

What happens if I leave Premium?

Nothing is deleted. Accounts and cards beyond the first two become read-only, and sync pauses until you come back.

How do I cancel?

On your iPhone: Settings › your name › Subscriptions › SPNT. If you’re on the yearly trial, cancel before the 7 days end and you won’t be charged.
