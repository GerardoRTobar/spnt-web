Subscription

How do I cancel my subscription?

Subscriptions are managed from your Apple account. On your iPhone, go to Settings → your name → Subscriptions → SPNT. You can also open apps.apple.com/account/subscriptions.

I switched iPhones and don’t see Premium

Open the plans screen in the app and tap “Restore purchases”. Use the same Apple Account you paid with.

Your data

How do I move my data to another iPhone?

Sign in with the same account on both devices. With Premium, your data syncs automatically.

How do I export my data?

From the app, you can export your transactions to Excel or CSV, or create a PDF report.

How do I delete my account?

In the app, go to Settings, tap your profile at the top and choose “Delete account”. Your account and your data in the cloud are deleted. More details in the privacy policy.

Does SPNT connect to my bank?

No. SPNT only knows what you record. It doesn’t ask for your bank usernames or passwords.
