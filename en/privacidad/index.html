Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 26, 2026

SPNT is an app for keeping track of your spending, accounts and cards. This policy explains what data the app handles, where it’s stored and what you can do with it.

The short version

  • We never sell your data or show ads, whether you use SPNT free or with Premium.
  • Your data is stored on your iPhone. If you sync, it travels encrypted to our servers. We don’t look at it, sell it or use it for ads.
  • We don’t use tracking or third-party analytics.
  • You can delete your account and your data from the app whenever you want.

In detail

1What’s stored on your iPhoneEverything you record is stored on your device.

Everything you record (transactions, accounts, cards, statements, categories, tags, loans, trips, recurring expenses and filters) is stored on your device. If you don’t create an account, none of it leaves your iPhone.

SPNT doesn’t connect to your bank or read your email. If you set up capture, the bank alerts and Apple Pay payments you hand it are read on your iPhone: the message never leaves the device, and only what you confirm becomes part of your data.

To import, you choose the file. If you use an AI (like ChatGPT or Claude) to put it together, that happens outside SPNT, under that AI’s rules: SPNT sends it nothing.

2If you create an accountIt’s optional. With Apple or Google, we receive your name, your email and an identifier.

An account is optional. If you sign in with Apple or Google, we receive your name, your email and an identifier for that account. With Apple you can hide your email: we then get an Apple address that forwards to yours, and we never see the real one. We also store your base currency and your language. When you create the account we send you a welcome email; we only write to you about your account.

3If you use sync (Premium)A copy of your data on our servers, which we don’t look at.

To have your data on multiple devices, a copy of what you record is stored on our servers. We use Supabase and PowerSync as providers, with servers in the United States. Your data is encrypted in transit, and each account can only read its own.

We don’t look at your data. We’d only access it if you ask us to for support, or if the law requires it.

4Subscriptions and paymentsProcessed by Apple. We never see your card details.

Payments are processed by Apple. We never see your card details. Apple notifies us of your subscription status (the plan, the price you paid, your store’s country, and whether it’s a trial, a renewal or a refund) so we can activate Premium on your account.

If you have an account, we also record how you use the plans screen: which screen was opened, which plan you chose and whether you tapped to buy. We use this only to improve that screen.

5SuggestionsWhat you write or vote for in the app, if you have an account.

If you have an account and send an idea or vote in Settings › Suggestions, we store what you write, your votes, your language and the app version, with your account. We use it only to decide what to improve, and it’s deleted with your account.

6What the app checks without an accountExchange rates, news and update notices, without sending your data.

Even without an account, the app downloads public exchange rates, SPNT news and a notice when there’s a new version from our servers. None of your data is sent with those requests; as with any internet connection, the server sees the IP address they come from.

7Face IDHandled by your iPhone. SPNT never receives your face.

Face ID is used only to show hidden amounts. It’s handled by your iPhone, and SPNT never receives your face or your biometric data.

8Who we work withThe providers that handle your data, and why.

Supabase also answers the requests without an account in section 6. The rest are only involved if you create an account, use sync or buy Premium.

9What we don’t doNo ads, no tracking, no selling data.
  • We don’t sell or share your data with advertisers.
  • We don’t show ads.
  • We don’t use tracking tools or third-party analytics tools.
10Delete or request your dataFrom Settings in the app, or by emailing us.

You can delete your account from the app: in Settings, tap your profile at the top and choose “Delete account”. This deletes your account and all the data stored on our servers. If you delete the app, the data on your iPhone is deleted. We only keep the purchase records that the law requires us to retain for accounting purposes. Those Apple purchase notifications are kept without your account (pseudonymised), so they are no longer linked to you.

You can also ask us for a copy of your data, or ask us to delete it, by emailing soporte@spnt.money.

11Your rightsAccess, copy, correction and deletion, and why we use each piece of data.

Why we use your data. To give you what you asked for (your account, sync and Premium), to improve the app (the plans screen and suggestions) and to comply with the law (purchase records).

For how long. As long as you have an account. When you delete it, your data is removed from our servers right away; purchase records are kept as long as the law requires.

What you can ask for. To see your data, get a copy, correct it, delete it or object to a use, by writing to soporte@spnt.money. We reply within 30 days. If you live in the European Union or another country with a data protection law, you can also complain to your country’s authority.

Where. Our providers have servers in the United States, so your data may be processed there, with the safeguards those providers offer for international transfers.

12ChildrenSPNT is not directed at children under 13 (16 in the EU).

SPNT is not directed at children under 13 (16 if you live in the European Union), and we do not knowingly collect data from them.

13Changes to this policyWe update the date and, if it’s important, we let you know.

If this policy changes, we’ll update the date above. If the change is important, we’ll let you know in the app.

Questions about your data?

We’ll answer you at soporte@spnt.money
