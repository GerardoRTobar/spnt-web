Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 28, 2026

SPNT is an app for keeping track of your spending, accounts and cards. This policy explains what data the app handles, where it’s stored and what you can do with it.

The short version

  • If you don’t create an account, your data doesn’t leave your iPhone.
  • We don’t sell your data or show ads.
  • We don’t use tracking or third-party analytics.
  • You can delete your account and your data from the app whenever you want.

In detail

1What’s stored on your iPhoneIf you don’t create an account, nothing leaves your device.

Everything you record (transactions, accounts, cards, statements, categories, tags, loans, trips, recurring expenses and filters) is stored on your device. If you don’t create an account, none of it leaves your iPhone.

SPNT doesn’t connect to your bank or read your email or notifications. It only knows what you record.

2If you create an accountIt’s optional. With Google, we receive your email and an identifier.

An account is optional. If you sign in with Google, we receive your email and an identifier for your Google account. We also store your base currency and your language.

3If you use sync (Premium)An encrypted copy of your data that only your account can see.

To have your data on multiple devices, a copy of what you record is stored on our servers. We use Supabase and PowerSync as providers, with servers in the United States. Your data is encrypted in transit, and only your account can see it.

4Subscriptions and paymentsProcessed by Apple. We never see your card details.

Payments are processed by Apple. We never see your card details. Apple notifies us of your subscription status (the plan, the price you paid, your store’s country, and whether it’s a trial, a renewal or a refund) so we can activate Premium on your account.

If you have an account, we also record how you use the plans screen: which screen was opened, which plan you chose and whether you tapped to buy. We use this only to improve that screen.

5Exchange ratesDownloaded without sending any of your data.

The app downloads public exchange rates. None of your data is sent with that request.

6Face IDHandled by your iPhone. SPNT never receives your face.

Face ID is used only to show hidden amounts. It’s handled by your iPhone, and SPNT never receives your face or your biometric data.

7Who we work withThe providers that handle your data, and why.

They’re only involved if you create an account, use sync or buy Premium.

ProviderPurpose
GoogleSign-in. Gives us your email and an identifier.
SupabaseYour account and the synced copy of your data. Servers in the United States.
PowerSyncSyncing your data across devices. Servers in the United States.
AppleCharging for the subscription and notifying us of its status.
8What we don’t doNo ads, no tracking, no selling data.
  • We don’t sell or share your data with advertisers.
  • We don’t show ads.
  • We don’t use tracking tools or third-party analytics tools.
9Delete or request your dataFrom Settings in the app, or by emailing us.

You can delete your account from the app: in Settings, tap your profile at the top and choose “Delete account”. This deletes your account and all the data stored on our servers. If you delete the app, the data on your iPhone is deleted. We only keep the purchase records that the law requires us to retain for accounting purposes.

You can also ask us for a copy of your data, or ask us to delete it, by emailing soporte@spnt.money.

10ChildrenSPNT is not directed at children under 13.

SPNT is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect data from them.

11Changes to this policyWe update the date and, if it’s important, we let you know.

If this policy changes, we’ll update the date above. If the change is important, we’ll let you know in the app.

Questions about your data?

We’ll answer you at soporte@spnt.money
