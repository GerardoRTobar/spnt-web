Automate transaction entry

Logging every purchase by hand? Ugh. Apple Pay and your bank’s alerts flow into SPNT on their own; you just confirm.

When your bank texts you or you pay with Apple Pay, SPNT reads it on your iPhone and leaves the transaction in “To review” with the amount, merchant, date and card. You confirm it with one tap. Nothing is saved without you seeing it.

You need SPNT 1.1 or later. Use one, two or all three. The steps are also in the app, under Settings › Data › Automations.

Methods

1Paste the textNothing to set up

Works on any iPhone, with nothing to set up.

  1. In Messages, touch and hold your bank’s alert and tap Copy.
  2. In SPNT, tap + and choose Paste text, at the top center. If what you copied has an amount, it says “Ready to paste”.
  3. Here’s how it’d look appears, with the amount, merchant, card and date. Tap Save, or Edit to change something.

If something still needs deciding, like the card, the button says Review and save and opens the filled-in form.

2Apple Pay, automatic1 minute, with a shortcut

Every Apple Pay payment comes in on its own.

Find your version in Settings › General › About.

With the ready-made shortcut

Add shortcut

Open it on your iPhone. Shortcuts opens with “Apple Pay spnt”.

  1. Tap Add Shortcut in the Shortcuts app.
  2. Open the “Apple Pay spnt” shortcut and tap the When … is used block. Pick your cards (the Wallet cards you want to log). Leave Automation on and Notify off.
  3. Tap ⓘ in the bottom bar › Privacy and turn on Allow Running When Locked and spnt.

Done. On your next Apple Pay payment you get “From Apple Pay: $12.50 at Merchant”. The first time with each card, SPNT asks which one it is; after that it’s picked for you.

Do it by hand on iOS 27If you’d rather build the shortcut yourself.
  1. In Shortcuts, tap + for a new shortcut and name it, for example “Apple Pay spnt”.
  2. Add the Wallet trigger (“When I tap my card or pass to pay…”). It also shows up in the Shortcuts search, under Automation. The block reads “When … is used”, with your cards in place of the dots.
  3. Add Action › search for spnt › Log Wallet purchase. In each field pick the payment’s variable, so it reads “Log Amount USD at Merchant with Card or Pass”.
  4. Tap the arrow on the “When … is used” block: Categories to Any Category, Merchants to Any Merchant, Automation on and Notify off. In the action, turn off Show When Run.
  5. ⓘ › Privacy: turn on Allow Running When Locked and spnt.

From Automation

On these versions Apple doesn’t let you share automations by link, so you build it by hand:

  1. In Shortcuts, open the Automation tab and tap +.
  2. Choose Wallet. Select your cards, leave any category, choose Run Immediately and tap Next.
  3. Tap New Blank Automation › Add Action › search for spnt › Log Wallet purchase.
  4. Tap each field and pick the payment’s data: the amount, the merchant and the card. Tap Done.
3Bank texts, automatic2 minutes, step by step

Every purchase alert your bank texts you comes in on its own. There’s no link for this one: the sender is different for every bank, so it takes 2 minutes to set up. If you have several banks, repeat it for each.

Find your version in Settings › General › About.

With a new shortcut

  1. In Shortcuts, tap + for a new shortcut and name it, for example “spnt bank texts”.
  2. Add the Message trigger (it also shows up in the Shortcuts search, under Automation). Under Sender, choose the number or name your bank texts you from.
  3. Add Action › search for spnt › Log from a message. Tap “Message” in the action and pick the Message variable.
  4. On the trigger block, leave Automation on and Notify off. In the action, turn off Show When Run.
  5. ⓘ › Privacy: turn on Allow Running When Locked and spnt.

From Automation

  1. In Shortcuts, open the Automation tab and tap +.
  2. Choose Message. Under Sender, choose your bank’s number or name. Choose Run Immediately and tap Next.
  3. Tap New Blank Automation › Add Action › search for spnt › Log from a message.
  4. Tap “Message” in the action and pick the Message variable (the shortcut input). Tap Done.

No need to filter: codes, promotions and other bank texts without an amount are ignored.

To test it, buy something with that card. When the text arrives you get “From a text: $12.50 at Merchant”, even with the iPhone locked.

Questions

Do my messages leave my iPhone?

No. SPNT reads the text on your iPhone, with no internet and no AI. Shortcuts only passes along messages from the sender you chose, not the rest. The text is kept only until you confirm or discard the draft, and drafts don’t sync: what syncs is the transaction you save, as always.

Is anything saved without me seeing it?

No. Everything comes in as a draft in “To review”. Tap it to check and save it, or swipe it to discard it. Home shows how many are waiting.

What if I pay in another currency?

If the bank’s alert names the currency, for example “EUR 30.00”, SPNT detects it and the form converts it at the day’s rate. The Apple Pay shortcut, though, always sends the amount in US dollars (USD), even if you paid in another currency: before saving, tap the draft and change the currency in the form.

What if the text and the Apple Pay payment are for the same purchase?

They end up as a single draft, with the merchant from Wallet and the card digits from the text, and you get one notification. If the text arrives more than 15 minutes after the payment you may get two: SPNT flags the second as a likely duplicate and you discard one.

Why does it ask which card it is?

To learn. Pick it once and the next purchases with that card come in with it. If the card isn’t in SPNT yet, “Create card” sets it up with the name and digits filled in.

If a card is replaced

With the first text from the new one, SPNT offers to change its last digits. If you use it with Apple Pay, the new card enters Wallet as a different card: also select it in the shortcut (“Apple Pay spnt” › “When … is used”; on iOS 18 to 26, in the Wallet automation). It only runs for the cards you chose.

Nothing is coming in
  • Check that Allow Running When Locked is on (iOS 27) or Run Immediately is chosen (iOS 18 to 26).
  • Check that the action is from spnt and, for texts, that the sender is your bank.
  • If the draft arrives but not the notification: check SPNT’s notifications in the iPhone’s Settings and the Notify me when something arrives switch in “To review”.

If one of your alerts is read wrong, send us the text at soporte@spnt.money (you can hide the name or authorization code) and we’ll add it.

How do I turn it off?

iOS 27: in Shortcuts, touch and hold the shortcut and tap Delete, or open it and turn off Automation on the trigger block.

iOS 18 to 26: in Shortcuts › Automation, tap the automation and turn it off, or swipe it left to delete it.

Whatever is already in “To review” stays there until you discard it; the ⋯ menu has “Discard all”. If you just don’t want the notifications, turn off Notify me when something arrives.
